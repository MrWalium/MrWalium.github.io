Unfazed Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11/17/26

Unfazed ("the app") is provided by William Marinin, US, contact: [SUPPORT EMAIL]. This policy explains what the app does with information. The app is intended for people 16 and older.

1. Summary

Your blocking settings, usage statistics and baseline stay on your phone. We have no accounts and do not receive your usage. The only data that leaves your device is what Google's advertising software collects when ads are shown, and a small public rules file the app may download. Details below.

2. Information stored on your device

The app stores on your device: your settings and schedules; how long you use the apps you chose to block, by day and time of day; counters such as pauses used; your baseline (the average daily time you gave or that was measured); and records that you accepted this policy. This information is not sent to us. You can delete it at any time using Settings > help & about > delete all data, or by uninstalling the app.

3. Accessibility service

To work, the app asks you to turn on Android's accessibility service. While it is on, the app can see which app is open and the names of buttons and tabs on screen (for example "Reels") in the apps you choose to block, and whether a text box is showing. It uses this to cover the screen so swipes do not scroll, and to send you back or to your home screen. It does not collect or store your messages, what you type, or passwords. It briefly checks the label of something you tap in a blocked app to decide whether to pass the tap through; nothing is saved. This information is not used for advertising or analytics and is not shared with anyone. You can turn the service off at any time in Android's accessibility settings.

4. Usage access (optional)

If you choose "measure from my phone" during setup, the app uses Android's Usage Access permission once to read how long you used the blocked apps over the last 7 days, to set your baseline. This stays on your device.

5. Ads

The app shows rewarded ads from Google AdMob when you take a pause. The Google Mobile Ads software on your device automatically collects and shares with Google information such as your Android advertising ID, IP address (which may be used to estimate your general location), interactions with ads, and diagnostic information, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and some US states), you are asked for your choices through Google's consent message, and you can change them under Settings > help & about > ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Google's use of data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

6. Notifications

The app can show notifications (for example "5 minutes left"). They are created on your device.

7. Your choices and rights

Because we do not hold your information, you can exercise most choices yourself: delete all data in the app, uninstall it, turn off accessibility or Usage Access in Android settings, and manage ad choices as above. If you are in the EEA, UK, California or another region with privacy rights, you may have rights to access, delete or object to processing of data we control. Email [SUPPORT EMAIL] and we will respond. For data Google collects through ads, contact Google.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Security and retention

Data stays on your device until you delete it. Ad data sent to Google is encrypted in transit.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a material way, the app will ask you to accept it again.
