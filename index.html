Unfazed

Unfazed helps you scroll less. It limits Reels, feeds and short videos in the social apps you choose, lets you take paced breaks, and keeps your chats usable.

Accessibility permission

Unfazed uses Android's accessibility service to notice which app is open and to hold your feed in place. It does not collect or store your messages, what you type, or passwords, and nothing it sees leaves your phone.
